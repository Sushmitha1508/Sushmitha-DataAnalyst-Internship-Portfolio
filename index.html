REFLECTION

Key Learnings

What carried across every stage of the project.

Data quality shapes every downstream decision.

Inconsistent customer names and duplicate order IDs, caught during cleaning, prevented flawed conclusions later on.

The "obvious" segmentation variable isn't always useful.

Clustering and a formal Chi-squared test (p = 0.526) both confirmed gender doesn't predict customer value — behavior does.

One retention statistic can reframe a strategy.

Finding that 94.5% of customers were one-time buyers shifted the focus from acquisition to retention.

Statistical rigor makes a narrative credible.

Pairing the segmentation story with a hypothesis test turned a pattern into a defensible business conclusion.
